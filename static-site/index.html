Adventure awaits.

Independent destinations to consider while staying in Kawartha Lakes. They are not services or amenities provided by LakeFront House.

01↕Historic waterway

Kirkfield Lift Lock

See Lock 36, the world’s second-highest hydraulic lift lock, with a lift of about 15 metres.

Official visitor information →
02△Provincial park

Balsam Lake Provincial Park

Plan a seasonal day of swimming, hiking, birding, boating or paddling at this Ontario park.

Official visitor information →
03≈Boating country

Trent–Severn Waterway

Explore a 386-kilometre historic waterway known for boating, paddling and its lockstations.

Official visitor information →
04⌁Conservation area

Pigeon River Headwaters

Walk nearly five kilometres of connected loops through forest, wetlands and meadows at the Pigeon River headwaters.

Official visitor information →
05⌣Provincial park

Emily Provincial Park

Enjoy seasonal swimming, boating, beginner-friendly canoeing and fishing along the Pigeon River.

Official visitor information →
06♧Multi-use forest

Ganaraska Forest

Explore hiking, mountain biking, horseback riding, cross-country skiing and snowshoeing trails. A day pass or membership is required.
