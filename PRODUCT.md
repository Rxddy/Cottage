# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Families and groups of up to ten guests comparing waterfront cottage stays in Kawartha Lakes. They need to understand the house, sleeping arrangements, rules, price and live availability before contacting the host.

## Product Purpose

Lakefront Serenity presents the actual property clearly and turns interest into a qualified booking request. Success means guests can picture the stay, trust the information, estimate their cost and contact the host without leaving the website.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, welcoming and quietly confident. The experience should feel like arriving at a well-kept family cottage: relaxed, useful and personal without pretending to be a luxury resort.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI landing-page copy, repetitive card grids, inflated travel language, stock-photo polish, glass-heavy interfaces, novelty animation and any layout that hides practical booking details.

## Design Principles

- Show the real house and lake before making claims about the experience.
- Keep dates, rates, guest limits and booking steps easy to find.
- Use motion to guide attention, never to slow down planning.
- Group long information by the questions guests actually ask.
- Preserve privacy by withholding the exact address and keypad code until confirmation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard and touch interactions equivalent, maintain clear focus states and readable contrast, and respect reduced-motion preferences. Booking controls must remain usable on current desktop and mobile browsers.
